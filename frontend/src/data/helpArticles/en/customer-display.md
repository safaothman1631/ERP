# Customer-facing display

A customer-facing display is a second screen pointed at the buyer that shows the running total, items being scanned, and the final amount. It's required by law in some markets and a trust booster everywhere else.

## Hardware options

1. **Browser tab on a second monitor.** Connect a 7" or 10" display via HDMI to your POS terminal, open `https://app.zoho-kurdish.iq/pos/display` in fullscreen.
2. **Tablet on Wi-Fi.** Use any Android or iOS tablet — open the same URL after pairing the tablet to the POS session from **Settings → Hardware → Customer display**.

## Pairing

1. On the POS terminal, go to **Settings → Hardware → Customer display**.
2. Click **Pair new display**. A QR code appears.
3. On the second screen, scan the QR code. The display is now paired with this terminal.

## What the customer sees

* Items as they are scanned with prices.
* Discounts applied.
* Subtotal, tax, total.
* "Thank you" message at the end of the sale.

You can upload a logo and pick the accent colour from the same settings screen.
