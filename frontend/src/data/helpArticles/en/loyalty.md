# Loyalty program

Loyalty awards repeat customers points based on their spend, which they can later redeem for discounts or free items.

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Settings → Loyalty**.
2. Set the earn rate (e.g. "1 point per 1000 IQD spent").
3. Set the redeem rate (e.g. "100 points = 1000 IQD discount").
4. Optionally cap how many points a single sale can burn.

## Enrol a customer

At checkout, tap **Customer → Enrol in loyalty**. Capture their name and phone — the phone is the membership key.

## Earn

Any tagged customer automatically accrues points on the sale total. The receipt shows points earned and the new balance.

## Redeem

Tap **Apply points** during checkout. The cashier sets how many points to burn; the discount line is added automatically.

## Reports

**Reports → Loyalty** shows total points outstanding (a liability) and the most-active members.
