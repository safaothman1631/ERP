# Recurring invoices

For subscription-style customers — gyms, software, rentals — Zoho Kurdish can generate invoices on a schedule.

## Set up a recurring schedule

1. From the customer record, click **More → New recurring invoice**.
2. Pick a frequency: weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly.
3. Choose a start date and (optionally) an end date.
4. Add the lines as on a normal invoice.
5. Choose what happens when the invoice is generated: save as draft, or save and send.

## How they generate

A scheduler runs every hour and emits new invoices that have fallen due. You don't need to log in for invoices to be created.

## Pause / resume

You can pause a recurring template at any time from the recurring list. The customer keeps existing invoices but new ones stop generating.

## Common issues

* **Invoice didn't generate** — check the recurring's "next run" date in the list; if blank, the schedule has ended.
* **Customer email bounced** — the bounce is logged on the customer record under **Activity**.
