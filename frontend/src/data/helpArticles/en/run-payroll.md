# Run payroll

Payroll is a monthly ritual. Zoho Kurdish makes it a four-click process.

## Walk-through

1. **Payroll → Pay runs → + New pay run**.
2. Pick the pay period (defaults to last month).
3. Review the employee list. Each row shows gross, allowances, deductions, tax, net.
4. Spot-check, then click **Compute**. The system applies withholding and social-security rules.
5. Click **Post** to commit. Journal entries are written, payslips are generated.

## Pay the employees

After posting, the **Pay** button generates a bank batch file (CSV or XML, depending on your bank). Upload it to your bank's portal to disburse salaries in one transaction.

## Off-cycle payments

For bonuses or off-cycle payments, click **+ Off-cycle**. The flow is identical but you pick specific employees.

## Common issues

* **Wrong tax computed** — check the employee's **Tax status** in their profile.
* **Net is negative** — deductions exceed gross. Correct the deduction (often a loan repayment too large) and re-compute.
