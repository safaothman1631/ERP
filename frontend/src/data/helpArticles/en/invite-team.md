# Invite your team

Zoho Kurdish supports unlimited users on every plan. Here's how to bring them in.

## Send an invite

1. Go to **Settings → Users**.
2. Click **Invite user** (top right).
3. Enter the person's email, choose a role, and click **Send invite**.

The invitee receives an email with a one-click link that expires in 7 days.

## Roles at a glance

* **Owner** — full control, including billing and user management.
* **Admin** — full control of business data, no billing.
* **Accountant** — books, taxes, reports.
* **POS cashier** — only POS terminal.
* **Viewer** — read-only.

You can also build custom roles from **Settings → Roles**.

## Bulk import

For more than 5 users, upload a CSV from **Settings → Users → Import**. The template is on the import screen.

## Common issues

* **Invite expired** — re-send it. Each invite is single-use.
* **User can't see modules** — their role lacks permission. Edit the role to grant the modules.
