# Your first login

After signing up, you arrive on the Zoho Kurdish home screen. Here's a quick tour so you know what's where.

## The shell

The application shell is split into three regions: a left sidebar with navigation, a top bar with search and user menu, and the main content area. In right-to-left languages (Kurdish, Arabic), the sidebar moves to the right.

## Navigation

The sidebar lists the modules you have access to — Invoicing, POS, Inventory, and so on. Modules you haven't enabled are hidden by default; activate them from **Settings → Modules**.

## Search

Press `Ctrl/Cmd + K` to open the universal search. Type the first few letters of any record, customer, or page name and we'll jump you there.

## User menu

Top-right shows your name and current company. Click it for shortcuts to:
* Profile and password
* Switching companies (multi-entity)
* Language selector (Kurdish / Arabic / English)
* Sign out

## Common issues

* **Sidebar items missing** — your role may not have permission to see them. Ask your admin to update permissions.
* **Text appears in the wrong direction** — change the language from the user menu.
