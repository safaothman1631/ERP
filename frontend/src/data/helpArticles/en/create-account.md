# Create your account

Welcome to Zoho Kurdish. This article walks you through creating an account in under two minutes.

## Before you begin

You need a working email address and a phone number that can receive WhatsApp or SMS. Iraq, Kurdistan Region, and most Middle-East countries are supported.

## Step by step

1. Open `https://app.zoho-kurdish.iq` in any modern browser. Chrome, Edge, and Safari all work.
2. Click **Create account** on the bottom-right of the login screen.
3. Type your business name in either Kurdish, Arabic, or English. You can change it later in **Settings → Company**.
4. Enter your email and choose a strong password — at least 8 characters with one capital letter and one digit.
5. Click **Send code**. We send a 6-digit verification code to your phone via WhatsApp.
6. Enter the code, accept the terms of service, and click **Sign up**.

Your tenant is now provisioned and you'll land on the **First sale** onboarding wizard.

## What's next

After signup, the onboarding wizard guides you through the five fastest steps to your first invoice. See *The 60-second onboarding wizard* for the full walkthrough.

## Common issues

* **Didn't receive the code** — wait 60 seconds, then click "Resend". If it still doesn't arrive, double-check the country code on your phone number.
* **Email already in use** — that email already has a tenant. Use **Sign in** instead, or contact support to recover access.
