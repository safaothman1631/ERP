# A tour of the system

Zoho Kurdish has 30+ modules. You don't need them all at once. Here's a guided tour of the ten most-used screens.

## Dashboard

The home screen shows the key KPIs for your business — today's sales, outstanding invoices, low-stock items, and recent activity.

## Invoices

The heart of the system. Create invoices, send them via email or WhatsApp, accept payments via Stripe or local providers.

## POS Terminal

A fast cashier-friendly point of sale. Works offline. Supports receipt printer, cash drawer, customer display.

## Inventory

Track items, stock levels, categories, batches, and transfers between locations.

## Bills

Record vendor bills, attach receipts, mark them paid from a bank account.

## Banking

Reconcile bank statements against booked transactions.

## Reports

Profit & loss, balance sheet, sales by product, expense breakdown.

## CRM

Manage leads, opportunities, deals, contacts.

## Projects

Track project tasks, time entries, and project profitability.

## Settings

Configure company info, locations, taxes, integrations, users, and feature flags.
