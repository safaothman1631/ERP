# Gift cards

A gift card is a stored-value voucher you sell that can be redeemed against future purchases. Zoho Kurdish supports physical cards (barcoded) and digital codes (sent by email or WhatsApp).

## Sell a gift card

1. From POS, tap **Gift card → Sell**.
2. Enter the load amount.
3. For a physical card, scan its barcode; for digital, the system generates a code and offers a print/SMS button.
4. Charge the customer the load amount.

The system posts the sale to a liability account — it's not revenue until redeemed.

## Redeem a gift card

1. At checkout, tap **Apply gift card**.
2. Scan the card or type the code.
3. The card's balance is applied as a tender; any remainder is paid in cash, card, etc.

## Balance lookup

Customers can check their balance at any cashier — tap **Gift card → Lookup**.
