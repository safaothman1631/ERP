# Create an invoice

Invoicing is the most-used screen in Zoho Kurdish. Here's how to make a new one.

## Open the form

From the sidebar, click **Invoicing → Invoices → + New invoice**.

## Fill the header

* **Customer** — pick an existing customer, or type a new name to quick-create.
* **Invoice number** — auto-assigned per your numbering rules.
* **Invoice date** and **due date**.
* **Reference number** — optional, for the customer's PO.

## Add line items

Click **Add line**. Each line accepts:

* Item — pick from inventory or type to quick-create.
* Quantity, rate, discount.
* Tax — auto-fills from the item or the customer's tax group.
* Account — auto-fills from the item.

## Review totals

The right-side panel shows subtotal, discount, tax, and total. If the customer is in a different currency, the FX panel shows the converted total at today's rate.

## Save and send

* **Save as draft** — keeps it editable.
* **Save and send** — locks the invoice and emails / WhatsApps it to the customer with a payment link.
* **Save as PDF** — for offline sending.

## Common issues

* **Tax row is missing** — the customer has no default tax group. Set one on the customer record.
* **"Stock not available"** — the inventory item is below zero. Either restock or sell as a service.
