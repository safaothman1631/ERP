# Year-end tax closing

Closing the tax year locks the books so prior-period entries can't be changed and prepares the data for tax filing.

## Pre-close checklist

* All invoices for the year posted and reconciled.
* Bills entered, paid or accrued.
* Bank reconciliations up to 31 Dec done.
* Inventory count complete; adjustments posted.
* Depreciation for the year booked.
* Payroll fully posted and withholding settled.

## Run the close

**Settings → Accounting → Close fiscal year**.

* Pick the closing date.
* Pick the retained-earnings account.
* Review the proposed close entries — net income closes to retained earnings.
* Click **Post**.

The books are locked. Re-opening requires the admin role and is audit-logged.

## File with the authority

* **VAT/sales tax**: quarterly returns from the Tax report.
* **Withholding**: annual filing.
* **Corporate income tax**: as per the schedule on the GCT website.

The reports under **Reports → Tax → Year-end** are formatted to match the official forms.
