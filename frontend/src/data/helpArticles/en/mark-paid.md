# Mark an invoice as paid

When you receive cash, a bank transfer, or any non-gateway payment, mark the invoice as paid so your books and AR aging stay correct.

## From the invoice

1. Open the invoice.
2. Click **Record payment**.
3. Pick the deposit account (cash, a bank, or undeposited funds).
4. Enter the amount (defaults to the full balance).
5. Choose a payment date and method.
6. Click **Save**.

A receipt PDF is generated and a notification fires to the customer.

## Partial payments

You can record several partial payments — each shows as a row on the invoice's payments section. The invoice closes when the total is fully covered.

## Over-payment

If the customer pays too much, you can keep the excess as a credit on the customer record. The credit auto-applies to future invoices.

## Common issues

* **Wrong account picked** — undo from **Payments → All payments → Edit**. The reversing entries are posted automatically.
