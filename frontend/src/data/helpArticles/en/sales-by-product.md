# Sales by product

This report answers: which items make me money?

## Open

**Reports → Sales → By product**.

Columns are revenue, COGS, gross profit, gross margin %, units sold.

## Sort and filter

Sort by margin to find the best products. Sort by units to find the popular ones. Filter to a single category to compare similar items.

## Slow movers

The **Slow movers** view shows items with declining unit sales — a candidate for promotion or delisting.

## Period comparison

Toggle **Compare to prior period** to see growth or decline per item.
