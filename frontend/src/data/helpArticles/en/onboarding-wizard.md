# The 60-second onboarding wizard

This wizard takes you from an empty database to a working first sale in five steps.

## Step 1 — Company info

Confirm your business name, address, and phone number. We need a valid Iraqi phone number to enable WhatsApp notifications later.

## Step 2 — Region

Pick the governorate you operate in. We pre-load the correct VAT and withholding rates for that region. You can later add more locations from **Settings → Locations**.

## Step 3 — Chart of accounts

Choose one of the five templates: General Trading, Restaurant, Services, Construction, or NGO. Each template installs around 60 accounts that match Iraqi accounting practice. You can rename or hide accounts later.

## Step 4 — POS hardware

If you sell at a physical counter, pair your receipt printer now. Most ESC/POS printers (Epson, Bixolon, Star) work over Bluetooth or USB. If you don't sell at a counter, click **Skip**.

## Step 5 — First sale

Create one product, one customer, and one sale to verify everything works end-to-end. The wizard pre-fills sensible defaults.

When you finish, you're dropped onto the dashboard with a working sample. From there it's a normal business workflow.

## Why a wizard?

We discovered that most users churned in the first 24 hours because the empty state was overwhelming. The wizard guarantees you have a working invoice + sale + customer before you have to make any decisions.
