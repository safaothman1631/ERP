# Withholding tax

Withholding tax is the portion the buyer keeps back when paying a supplier or salary, on behalf of the tax authority. Iraq applies it on salaries (income tax brackets) and on certain B2B services.

## Set up

**Settings → Taxes → Withholding**. Configure:

* **Salary brackets** — income, social security, dependents allowance.
* **Vendor withholding** — flat rate or per-vendor.

We pre-load Iraq's brackets and KRG's variations.

## On payroll

When you run payroll, the computed gross is split into:

* Net to employee.
* Income tax withheld.
* Social security withheld.
* Employer contributions.

Each portion posts to its own liability account and is settled when you pay the tax authority.

## On vendor bills

If a vendor is flagged "withhold tax", the bill shows the withheld amount as a deduction. The vendor receives the net; the held amount goes to a liability account and is paid later to the authority.

## Reports

**Reports → Withholding** shows quarterly summaries ready for the tax authority's quarterly filing.
