# Profit & loss report

The P&L (also called the income statement) shows revenue, expenses, and net profit for a period.

## Open the report

**Reports → P&L**.

* Pick the date range — month, quarter, year, or custom.
* Pick comparison — vs prior period, vs prior year, vs budget.
* Pick the basis — accrual (default) or cash.

## Drill down

Each line is clickable. Clicking "Revenue" opens a breakdown by product. Clicking a product opens the invoices behind that number. The drill goes all the way to individual journal entries.

## Filters

* **Location** — for multi-location businesses.
* **Department** or **project** — for cost-center analysis.
* **Currency** — show base currency, or a specific foreign currency at the period's rate.

## Export

The **Export** button produces PDF, Excel, or CSV. Excel preserves the drill links as separate tabs.

## Schedule

Click **Schedule** to email the P&L to a list on the 1st of each month.
