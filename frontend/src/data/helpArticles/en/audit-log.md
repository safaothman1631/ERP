# Audit log

The audit log is the immutable trail of who did what, when. It's append-only and visible to admins.

## Open

**Settings → Audit log**.

## What's logged

* Sign-ins and sign-outs.
* Every create/update/delete of an entity (invoice, item, customer, etc.).
* Permission grants.
* Impersonation sessions.
* Configuration changes.

## Filter

By user, date range, entity type, or action.

## Export

PDF or CSV. Useful for auditor visits.

## Retention

Audit entries are retained 5 years by default. Customers on the Enterprise plan can extend this.
