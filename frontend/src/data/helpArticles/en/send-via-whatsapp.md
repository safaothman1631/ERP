# Send invoice via WhatsApp

WhatsApp is how most Iraqi businesses communicate. Zoho Kurdish integrates with WhatsApp Business so invoices, receipts, and reminders go through the same channel.

## One-time setup

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → WhatsApp**.
2. Pair your number with our 360Dialog account (we walk you through).
3. Pick a default template — we provide pre-approved ones.

## Send an invoice

From the invoice, click **Send → WhatsApp**. Pick the template; the system substitutes customer name, total, due date, and pay-link. Preview, then click **Send**.

The recipient gets a regular WhatsApp message with the pay-link.

## Templates

Pre-approved templates we ship:

* `invoice_new` — first-time delivery.
* `invoice_reminder` — payment reminder.
* `invoice_overdue` — overdue notice.
* `receipt_paid` — after payment.

Custom templates require approval from Meta — submit through the WhatsApp settings page.

## Common issues

* **"Template not approved"** — submitting a new template takes Meta 1–2 days.
* **"24-hour window closed"** — outside the conversation window, only template messages work. Send a template instead of free text.
