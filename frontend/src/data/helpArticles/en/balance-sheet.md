# Balance sheet

The balance sheet shows assets, liabilities, and equity at a point in time. It's the snapshot that proves the books balance.

## Open the report

**Reports → Balance sheet**.

* Pick the **as-of** date.
* Pick comparison — vs prior period or vs prior year.

## Sections

* **Assets** — cash, receivables, inventory, fixed assets.
* **Liabilities** — payables, taxes payable, loans.
* **Equity** — paid-in capital, retained earnings, current-year profit.

By the accounting identity, assets = liabilities + equity. If not, the system flags it red and points to the offending account.

## Common issues

* **Out of balance** — usually a manually-edited journal entry. The hint at the bottom tells you which.
* **Negative cash** — uncleared payments. Reconcile and reclassify.
