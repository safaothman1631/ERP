# Low-stock alerts

A low-stock alert tells you when an item is running out so you can reorder before you stock out.

## Set a threshold

On the item record, fill in:

* **Reorder level** — alert when on-hand ≤ this number.
* **Reorder quantity** — how much to buy back.
* **Preferred vendor** — to pre-fill the PO.

## See alerts

The **Inventory → Low stock** report lists every item at or below its reorder level. The dashboard widget surfaces the top 5 critical items.

## Auto-create POs

Tap **Create PO from alerts** to generate one purchase order per preferred vendor with the reorder quantities pre-filled.

## Bulk update

To set thresholds on many items at once, export to CSV from **Items → Export**, edit, and re-import.
