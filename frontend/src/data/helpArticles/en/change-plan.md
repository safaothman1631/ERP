# Change billing plan

Upgrade or downgrade your Zoho Kurdish plan from the billing screen.

## Open

**Settings → Billing → Plan**.

## Upgrade

Pick a higher tier and confirm. The new features unlock immediately. Charges are prorated for the remainder of the current billing period; the next invoice reflects the new monthly cost.

## Downgrade

Pick a lower tier. Restricted features stay accessible until the end of the current period, then lock. Any data created on a higher tier is preserved.

## Cancel

**Settings → Billing → Cancel plan**. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period. Until then everything keeps working. After cancellation, data is retained for 30 days during which you can reactivate; after 30 days, data is purged.

## Multi-currency billing

If your account is billed in USD but you collect IQD locally, the invoice shows both for clarity. We use the central-bank rate of the billing day.

## Common issues

* **Card declined** — update payment method and retry from the failed invoice.
* **Plan locked** — your account is in past-due status; settle the outstanding invoice first.
