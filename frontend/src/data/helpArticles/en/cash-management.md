# Cash drawer management

The cash drawer is the physical box under your POS that holds cash takings. Zoho Kurdish tracks every cash event so end-of-shift counting is fast.

## Open at start of shift

When you sign in to POS, the system prompts for an opening float (the cash you put in before any sales). Type the amount and confirm.

## Add or remove cash mid-shift

For cash put in or taken out for any reason — change for a sale, paying a delivery person — use the **Cash in / Cash out** button. Enter the amount and a reason. Both are journalled.

## Close at end of shift

Tap **End of shift** to count down. Type each denomination's count; we calculate the expected closing balance and the variance.

If variance is significant, the system can require a manager override before closing.

## Common issues

* **Drawer won't pop** — wrong cash-drawer pin. See *Pair a receipt printer*.
* **Variance is wrong by a round number** — usually a forgotten cash-in. Check the cash movements log.
