# Add an item

Items are the things you sell — products or services. Each item carries a price, a unit, and accounting defaults.

## Open the form

**Inventory → Items → + New item**.

## Required fields

* **Name** — what the customer sees on the receipt and invoice.
* **Type** — Goods (stocked, has stock movements) or Service (not stocked).
* **Sales price** and **purchase price** in your base currency.
* **Sales account** — usually "Revenue".
* **Income/COGS** — for stocked items, the account hit when stock leaves.

## Optional fields

* **SKU** and **barcode** — for scanning at POS.
* **Tax group** — overrides the customer's default.
* **KDS station** — for restaurants.
* **Categories**, **tags**, **brand**.
* **Image** — shown on POS tiles and the customer display.

## Pricing tiers

For wholesale/retail/employee discounts, define **price lists** under **Settings → Price lists** and attach customers or POS terminals to them.

## Common issues

* **Item not found on POS** — check the active price list includes the item, and the location's inventory has it.
