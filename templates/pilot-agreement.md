## English

### 1. Purpose
This Agreement governs a **30-day** (extendable to 90 by mutual written consent) free pilot of the Kurdish ERP platform ("Service") so the Customer can evaluate it in live daily operations and the Provider can gather product feedback.

### 2. What the Provider gives
1. **Free use** of the Service for the pilot term — all modules in the Customer's plan tier, no licence fee.
2. **Hardware on loan** for the pilot term: the kit listed in §0 (thermal printer, barcode scanner, tablet, cash drawer as applicable). The hardware remains Provider property and is returned at pilot end unless the Customer converts and elects to purchase it.
3. **Support commitments** (the SLA in §6).
4. **Weekly releases** to a dedicated pilot environment, with the Customer's feedback feeding directly into them.
5. **On-site setup and training** for the owner plus one staff member at Day 0.
6. **Data export at any time** on request, in a standard, machine-readable format, at no charge.

### 3. What the Customer commits to
1. **Use the Service as the primary** point-of-sale / business system for the pilot business during the term (not in parallel with a competing system except for a short cut-over).
2. **Accept weekly releases** to the pilot environment (the Provider gives advance notice of anything user-visible).
3. **Complete the weekly survey** (~5 minutes) and the day-14 and day-30 NPS/CSAT surveys.
4. **Take one ~30-minute founder/support call per week**, and short daily check-ins during the first 14 days.
5. **Report issues promptly** so they can be triaged and fixed.
6. **Safeguard the loaned hardware** with ordinary care.

### 4. Data ownership & privacy
1. The Customer **owns all business data** entered into the Service. The Provider processes it only to operate the Service and improve it, under the Data Processing Agreement (`/legal/dpa`, **DRAFT pending counsel**).
2. The Provider will **not sell** Customer data and will not use individual customer/transaction records for any purpose beyond operating the Service and the agreed feedback loop.
3. On request, at any time, the Provider provides a **full data export**; on pilot end or termination, the Customer may request **deletion** (executed within the grace period stated in the Privacy Policy).
4. Personal data of the Customer's own customers (e.g. ration-card or loyalty data) is handled by the Customer as controller; the Provider is a processor.

### 5. Case-study & reference rights
1. The Provider may produce a **case study** about the pilot. The Customer has the **right to review and redact** it before any publication; nothing is published without the Customer's written approval.
2. The Customer agrees to act as a **sales reference** (reference calls / a logo on the website) **only if** they opt in at exit; there is **no reference exclusivity** — the Provider may also reference other customers.
3. A pilot may elect to remain **confidential** (no public naming); the Provider will still use anonymized, aggregated learnings.

### 6. Support SLA (issue triage)
| Severity | Meaning | Commitment |
|----------|---------|------------|
| **P0** | Cannot transact / data loss / money wrong | **Same-day** fix or escalation |
| **P1** | Major feature broken | **24 hours** |
| **P2** | Feature broken, workaround exists | **1 week** |
| **P3** | Annoyance / wishlist | **1 month** |

- **Hardware replacement:** a failed loaned device is replaced within **48 hours** in-region where logistically possible.
- Open **P0+P1 backlog is kept under 3** at any time for this pilot.

### 7. Fees
The pilot is **free**. There are no charges during the term. Payment-gateway transaction fees charged by third parties (e.g. card networks) — if the Customer chooses to accept electronic payment during the pilot — are the Customer's responsibility and are disclosed before activation.

### 8. Conversion (graduation)
At or before Day 30 (or Day 90 if extended) either party may propose conversion to a paid plan. If the Customer converts, the Provider offers a **40% discount on the year-1 subscription** (anchor per ADR-112). Conversion is **optional**; declining carries no penalty.

### 9. Term & termination
1. The pilot runs for the term in §0.
2. **Either party may terminate at any time, for any reason, with no penalty**, by written notice (WhatsApp message to the agreed contact suffices).
3. On termination: the Customer returns the loaned hardware within **7 days**; the Provider provides a final data export on request; data is deleted on request per §4.

### 10. Liability & warranties (pilot-grade)
1. The Service is provided **"as is"** for the pilot. It is pre-general-availability software under active iteration.
2. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the Provider's aggregate liability arising out of the pilot is limited to **the value of the hardware on loan**. (There are no subscription fees during the pilot to anchor a higher cap.)
3. Nothing limits liability that cannot be limited under Iraqi law.
4. **The Customer remains responsible for its own legal and tax compliance** (e.g. VAT filing, e-Fakhata submission). The Service assists but does not assume the Customer's statutory obligations.

### 11. Confidentiality
Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential and uses it only for the pilot. Pricing, roadmap, and unreleased features shared during the pilot are Provider-confidential.

### 12. Governing law & disputes
Governed by the laws of **the Republic of Iraq / the Kurdistan Region as applicable** (**final choice pending counsel, T-SF.2.x**). The parties first attempt good-faith resolution; **[dispute-resolution venue — pending counsel]**.

| **P0** | تعذّر المعاملة / فقدان بيانات / خطأ في المال | إصلاح **في نفس اليوم** أو تصعيد |
| **P1** | عطل في ميزة رئيسية | **٢٤ ساعة** |
| **P2** | عطل في ميزة مع وجود حل بديل | **أسبوع** |
| **P3** | إزعاج / قائمة أمنيات | **شهر** |

- **استبدال الأجهزة:** يُستبدل الجهاز المُعار المعطّل خلال **٤٨ ساعة** ضمن المنطقة حيثما أمكن.
- يبقى عدد المشكلات المفتوحة من **P0+P1 أقل من ٣**.

### ٧. الرسوم
التجربة **مجانية**. لا رسوم خلال المدة. رسوم معاملات بوابة الدفع التي تفرضها أطراف ثالثة هي مسؤولية العميل.

### ٨. التحوّل (التخرّج)
في اليوم ٣٠ أو قبله، يجوز لأي طرف اقتراح التحوّل إلى خطة مدفوعة. إذا تحوّل العميل، يقدّم المزوّد **خصم ٤٠٪ على اشتراك السنة الأولى**. التحوّل **اختياري**.

### ٩. المدة والإنهاء
١. تستمر التجربة للمدة المذكورة في القسم ٠.
٢. **يجوز لأي طرف الإنهاء في أي وقت، لأي سبب، دون غرامة**، بإشعار خطي.
٣. عند الإنهاء: يعيد العميل الأجهزة المُعارة خلال **٧ أيام**؛ ويوفّر المزوّد تصديرًا نهائيًا للبيانات عند الطلب.

### ١٠. المسؤولية والضمانات
١. تُقدَّم الخدمة **"كما هي"** للتجربة.
٢. إلى أقصى حد يسمح به القانون، تقتصر المسؤولية الإجمالية للمزوّد على **قيمة الأجهزة المُعارة**.
٣. لا شيء يحدّ من المسؤولية التي لا يمكن تحديدها بموجب القانون العراقي.
٤. **يبقى العميل مسؤولًا عن امتثاله القانوني والضريبي** (مثل تقديم ضريبة القيمة المضافة وإرسال e-Fakhata).

### ١١. السرّية
يحافظ كل طرف على المعلومات غير العامة للطرف الآخر.

### ١٢. القانون الحاكم والنزاعات
