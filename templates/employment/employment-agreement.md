## 1. Position and duties

1.1 The Company employs the Employee as **`[JOB TITLE]`**, reporting to `[MANAGER/ROLE]`.

1.2 The Employee will perform the duties customary to the role and other reasonable duties assigned, devoting their full working time and skill to the Company during working hours, and acting in the Company's best interests.

1.3 **Place of work.** `[Erbil office / hybrid]`. The Company operates a **2-days-in-office / 3-days-remote** norm (with a shared Erbil co-working space); full-remote may be permitted for senior engineers after 6 months' tenure, at the Company's discretion. `[Adjust per role.]`

## 2. Commencement and probation

2.1 Employment begins on **`[START DATE]`**.

2.2 The first **`[3 months — CONFIRM statutory maximum]`** are a **probationary period**, during which either party may terminate on `[shorter statutory notice — CONFIRM]`. On successful completion, employment continues under this Agreement.

## 3. Term

3.1 This is `[an indefinite-term / a fixed-term ending [DATE]]` contract. `[Iraqi labour law treats indefinite and fixed-term contracts differently — counsel to confirm renewal/conversion rules.]`

## 4. Working hours

4.1 Normal working hours are `[hours/week — CONFIRM statutory maximum]`, `[days]`, consistent with applicable Iraqi labour law, with a weekly rest day (`[Friday]`). Overtime, where required and lawful, is compensated or time-off-in-lieu granted per law.

4.2 The Company observes official public holidays and adjusts hours during **Ramadan** in accordance with law and operational needs.

## 5. Remuneration and benefits

5.1 **Salary.** Gross salary of **`[amount] [IQD/USD]` per `[month]`**, payable monthly in arrears by bank transfer, **less lawful deductions** (income-tax withholding and social-security contributions where applicable).

5.2 **Social security.** The Company registers the Employee and remits social-security contributions as required by Iraqi law.

5.3 **Annual leave.** `[N]` working days of paid annual leave per year `[CONFIRM statutory minimum]`, accruing pro-rata, plus public holidays.

5.4 **Sick leave.** Paid sick leave per applicable Iraqi labour law, subject to medical certification.

5.5 **Other leave.** Maternity, paternity, bereavement, and other statutory leave per applicable law.

5.6 **Expenses.** Reasonable, pre-approved business expenses are reimbursed against receipts.

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Employee will keep confidential all non-public information of the Company, its customers, and partners, during and after employment, and use it only to perform their duties. This includes customer data, source code, business plans, financials, and security information.

6.2 On termination, the Employee returns or deletes all Company materials and confidential information in their possession.

## 7. Intellectual property

7.1 The Employee assigns to the Company all rights in work product created in the course of employment, as set out in the separate **IP Assignment Agreement** (`templates/employment/ip-assignment.md`), which the Employee signs at offer-acceptance and which is incorporated by reference.

7.2 To the extent any rights do not vest automatically, the Employee agrees to assign them and to sign documents reasonably required to perfect the Company's ownership.

## 8. Data protection and security

8.1 The Employee will comply with the Company's security and data-protection policies (including `docs/security/pii-handling.md` and the Acceptable Use Policy), handle personal data lawfully, and complete required security and incident-command training (repeated annually).

## 9. Conduct and policies

9.1 The Employee will comply with the Company's lawful policies and code of conduct as updated from time to time. Material policies are made available to the Employee.

## 10. Restrictive covenants

10.1 **Non-solicitation.** For **twelve (12) months** after termination, the Employee will not solicit the Company's employees or actively solicit its customers with whom the Employee dealt, to the extent enforceable under applicable Iraqi law.

10.2 **Non-compete.** `[Any non-compete must be narrowly tailored (scope, duration, geography) and may be unenforceable or limited under Iraqi labour law — COUNSEL TO CONFIRM. Default: omit or keep minimal.]`

## 11. Termination

11.1 After probation, either party may terminate on **`[statutory notice period — CONFIRM]`** written notice, or payment in lieu where lawful.

11.2 The Company may terminate **for cause** (serious misconduct) without notice, to the extent permitted by Iraqi labour law and following any required procedure.

11.3 On termination, the Company pays accrued salary, untaken statutory leave, and any **end-of-service entitlements** required by Iraqi labour law. `[Confirm severance/end-of-service formula with counsel.]`

11.4 Sections 6, 7, 10, and 12 survive termination.

## 12. Governing law and disputes

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the labour laws of the **Republic of Iraq** (and the **Kurdistan Region** where applicable). Disputes are resolved through the competent labour authority/court, and through any mandatory conciliation process required by law.

## 13. Entire agreement

13.1 This Agreement and the IP Assignment Agreement are the entire agreement on the subject and supersede prior understandings. Amendments must be in writing and signed, and may not reduce statutory minimum entitlements.
