# Variant A — Employee IP Assignment

## Parties

This IP Assignment Agreement ("**Agreement**") is between **`[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]`** (the "**Company**") and **`[EMPLOYEE FULL NAME]`** (the "**Employee**"), entered into in connection with the Employee's employment (the "**Employment Agreement**"), on `[DATE]`.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Work Product"** means all inventions, software, code, designs, documentation, data models, content, know-how, and other works that the Employee conceives, creates, or reduces to practice, alone or with others, **in the course of employment** or using the Company's resources, time, or Confidential Information.

1.2 **"Prior IP"** means intellectual property the Employee owned or created **before** employment or **outside** the scope of employment, listed in **Schedule 1**.

## 2. Assignment of Work Product

2.1 The Employee **irrevocably assigns to the Company** all worldwide right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all intellectual-property rights therein, effective on creation. To the extent any right does not vest automatically by law, the Employee assigns it and agrees to execute documents to perfect the assignment.

2.2 The Work Product is "work made for hire" to the extent that concept applies; where it does not, §2.1 governs.

## 3. Moral rights

3.1 To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the Employee **waives** (or agrees not to assert against the Company) moral rights in the Work Product, including rights of attribution and integrity, so the Company may use, modify, and distribute the Work Product. `[Iraqi law may treat moral rights as inalienable — COUNSEL TO CONFIRM the permissible waiver/consent scope.]`

## 4. Prior IP and third-party IP

4.1 The Employee lists all Prior IP in **Schedule 1**. If none, the Employee writes "None". The Employee will **not** incorporate Prior IP or any third-party IP into the Work Product without the Company's written consent; if incorporated, the Employee grants the Company a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide licence to use it as part of the Work Product.

4.2 The Employee warrants the Work Product will be original and will not knowingly infringe third-party rights.

## 5. Disclosure and cooperation

5.1 The Employee will promptly disclose Work Product to the Company.

5.2 During and **after** employment, the Employee will, at the Company's expense, sign documents and provide reasonable assistance to obtain, perfect, and enforce the Company's rights (post-termination cooperation). The Employee appoints the Company as attorney-in-fact to execute such documents if the Employee is unavailable, to the extent permitted by law.

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Employee's confidentiality obligations in the Employment Agreement §6 apply to this Agreement.

## 7. Non-solicitation

7.1 For **twelve (12) months** after termination, the Employee will not solicit the Company's employees or actively solicit its customers with whom the Employee dealt, to the extent enforceable under applicable Iraqi law (consistent with Employment Agreement §10).

## 8. General

8.1 This Agreement survives termination of employment. It is governed by the laws of the **Republic of Iraq** (and the **Kurdistan Region** where applicable). If a provision is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect.

# Variant B — Contractor / Consultant IP Assignment

## Parties

This IP Assignment Agreement is between **`[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]`** (the "**Company**") and **`[CONTRACTOR NAME]`**, `[entity/individual, registration if any]` (the "**Contractor**"), in connection with the services agreement dated `[DATE]` (the "**Services Agreement**").

## 1. Scope of assignment

1.1 The Contractor assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest in the **deliverables and work product created under the Services Agreement** ("**Deliverables**"), including all intellectual-property rights, on the terms below. Assignment is **project-/service-scoped** to the engagement, not to the Contractor's general work.

## 2. Payment-conditional vesting

2.1 Assignment of IP in the Deliverables **vests upon full payment** of the corresponding fees. Until paid, the Company holds a licence to use the Deliverables for evaluation and the engagement's purpose. On full payment, §1.1 assignment is effective retroactively to creation.

## 3. Background IP and licence-back

3.1 **"Background IP"** means IP the Contractor owned or developed independently of the engagement. The Contractor retains Background IP and grants the Company a **perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide, sublicensable licence** to use Background IP **to the extent embedded in the Deliverables** and necessary to exploit them.

## 4. Third-party IP and indemnity

4.1 The Contractor will not incorporate third-party IP into the Deliverables without the Company's written consent and a sufficient licence.

4.2 The Contractor **indemnifies** the Company against third-party claims that the Deliverables (excluding Company-supplied materials) infringe that third party's intellectual-property rights, subject to the liability terms of the Services Agreement.

## 5. Moral rights, confidentiality, disclosure

5.1 The Contractor waives (or agrees not to assert) moral rights in the Deliverables to the extent permitted by applicable law `[confirm with counsel]`; keeps the Company's information confidential; promptly discloses Deliverables; and provides reasonable post-engagement cooperation (at the Company's expense) to perfect the Company's rights.

## 6. Independent contractor

6.1 The Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee; nothing creates an employment or agency relationship.

## 7. General

7.1 Governed by the laws of the **Republic of Iraq** (and the **Kurdistan Region** where applicable). Survives termination of the Services Agreement. Severability applies.
