## 1. Who we are

We are `[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]`, an Iraq-domiciled company providing the Zoho Kurdish ERP / ERPIQ platform. For questions about this policy or to exercise your rights, contact **privacy@zoho.kurd.iq** or write to our registered office (§11).

This policy explains how we handle personal data **when we are the controller** — for example, account-administration data, billing data, marketing-site analytics, and support communications. When you use the Service to process personal data about *your* contacts and employees, that processing is governed by the DPA, and you decide the purposes and means.

## 2. What data we collect

| Category | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Account information** | Name, work email, phone, business name, role, organization (`org_id`), hashed password (Argon2id), 2FA settings | You, at sign-up and in settings |
| **Business records (as controller of admin data)** | Subscription plan, billing contact, invoices we issue to you, support entitlements | You / generated by the Service |
| **Usage & device data** | Last-login timestamp, IP address (hashed: `SHA-256(IP + per-tenant salt)`), user-agent, route navigation, feature events, RUM Web Vitals | Collected automatically |

| **Marketing data** | Lead-form submissions, email/WhatsApp opt-in status with timestamp, campaign attribution | You / our marketing site |
| **Cookies & similar** | See the Cookie Policy (`legal/cookie-policy.md`) | Your browser |

We follow **data minimization** (PDPL Art. 12 / GDPR Art. 5(1)(c)): our data models reject unknown fields and store only what a business purpose requires. We do **not** collect special-category data for our own controller purposes, and we do not store full payment-card numbers — only a processor token and the last four digits.

## 3. Why we process it (lawful basis)

| Purpose | Lawful basis (PDPL / GDPR) |
|---|---|
| Provide, operate, secure, and support the Service; manage your account | **Performance of a contract** (PDPL Art. 6 / GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Bill you, collect payment, comply with tax and accounting law | **Legal obligation** + contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b),(c)) |
| Secure the Service, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain audit logs | **Legitimate interests** (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) — keeping the Service and tenants safe |
| Product analytics and performance telemetry (RUM, anonymized) | **Legitimate interests**, balanced against your rights; non-essential analytics cookies require **consent** |
| Marketing emails / WhatsApp messages | **Consent** (PDPL Art. 7 / GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), revocable at any time |

Where we rely on legitimate interests, you may object (§7). Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting prior processing.

## 4. How long we keep it (retention)

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purpose collected, then delete or anonymize it. Indicative periods (aligned to our internal retention schedule and `docs/security/pii-handling.md`):

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account data | For the life of the account, then deleted within the **30-day** grace window after closure (§6) |
| Billing & tax records | **7 years** (Iraqi commercial-books requirement) |
| Security-relevant audit logs (auth, admin, billing, export events) | **≥ 18 months**, hash-chained for tamper evidence; cold-storage sink after 30 days |
| Usage telemetry (raw, identifiable) | **90 days**, then rolled up to anonymous aggregates |
| Backups | Daily for 30 days, weekly for 12 weeks, monthly for 12 months, yearly for 7 years; deleted data is overwritten on the normal backup rotation |
| Support communications | `[24 months]` after case closure |

## 5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it with:

- **Sub-processors** that help us run the Service (hosting, error monitoring, payments, messaging). The current list, with categories and regions, is maintained at **`legal/sub-processors.md`** and published at `/legal/sub-processors`. We impose data-protection terms on every sub-processor.
- **Professional advisors** (lawyers, accountants, auditors) under confidentiality.
- **Authorities**, where required by valid legal process or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the Service.
- **Successors**, in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to this policy.

## 6. Where your data goes (international transfers)

Operational data is hosted on **Google Cloud Firestore** with the **primary region `me-central1` (Doha, Qatar)** and **cross-region backup to `europe-west4` (Netherlands)**; a pre-warmed disaster-recovery standby exists in `asia-southeast1`. Our marketing site and static assets are served by **Vercel** (global edge); error telemetry is processed by **Sentry** (EU).

Because some sub-processors are located outside Iraq and the EEA, transfers may occur. For personal data subject to GDPR, we rely on the **European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs, 2021)** and the data-importer's own safeguards (e.g., Google Cloud's and Vercel's DPAs incorporating SCCs). For PDPL, we disclose these transfers here and apply equivalent contractual safeguards. **Customers requiring in-country or `me-central1`-only residency** should contact us; residency options are addressed in the DPA and MSA.

## 7. Your rights

Subject to PDPL and, where applicable, GDPR (Articles 15–22), you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **Rectify** inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **Erase** ("right to be forgotten") your data, subject to legal-retention exceptions;
- **Restrict** or **object to** processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing;
- **Data portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON, UTF-8, ISO-8601 dates);
- **Withdraw consent** at any time for consent-based processing;
- **Lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority (the Iraqi data-protection authority, or your EU/EEA supervisory authority).

We do not subject you to solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects.

## 8. How to exercise your rights

- **In-app:** Account administrators can export and request deletion of tenant and user data from the privacy/admin settings. Export is delivered as a signed-URL download (target turnaround **24 hours**); deletion runs on a **30-day soft-delete grace** then hard delete, with a deletion certificate issued as a receipt.
- **Email:** **privacy@zoho.kurd.iq** — we respond within **30 days** (PDPL/GDPR statutory window), extendable once where permitted for complex requests.
- **Withdraw a deletion** within the grace window by emailing privacy@zoho.kurd.iq.
- **Post:** `[REGISTERED ADDRESS]`.

We may need to verify your identity before acting.

## 9. Children

The Service is intended for businesses and is **not directed at individuals under 18**. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child's data has been provided to us, contact privacy@zoho.kurd.iq and we will delete it.

## 10. Security

We apply technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk (detailed in `legal/dpa.md`, Annex II), including: TLS 1.2+ in transit; field-level encryption at rest for direct identifiers (envelope encryption) and encryption at rest for all stored data; Argon2id password hashing; role-based access control and per-tenant isolation; hash-chained, tamper-evident audit logs; least-privilege access; backups with PITR; and an incident-response process with breach notification (24 hours to affected controllers where we are processor; 72 hours to the competent authority where we are controller).

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.
