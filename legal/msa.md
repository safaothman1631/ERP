## 1. Definitions

- **"Order Form"** — an ordering document executed by both parties specifying the Services, fees, quantities, and Subscription Term.
- **"Services"** — the Zoho Kurdish ERP / ERPIQ subscription services and any professional services identified in an Order Form.
- **"Subscription Term"** — the period stated in an Order Form during which Customer may use the Services.
- **"Customer Data"**, **"Personal Data"**, **"Confidential Information"** — as defined here and in the DPA (`legal/dpa.md`).
- **"Documentation"** — the then-current product documentation we make generally available.

## 2. Order Forms govern

2.1 Each Order Form is governed by this MSA. In case of conflict between an Order Form and this MSA, **the Order Form prevails** for that order only, and only where it expressly references the provision it overrides.

2.2 Order Forms may be executed by the parties' authorized signatories (including electronically). A purchase order's pre-printed terms have no effect.

## 3. Provision of the Services; licence

3.1 Provider will make the Services available to Customer per the applicable Order Form, this MSA, the **SLA** (Exhibit A), and the Documentation.

3.2 Subject to this MSA, Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, worldwide right during each Subscription Term to access and use the Services for Customer's internal business operations, up to the quantities in the Order Form.

3.3 **Restrictions.** Customer will not (a) resell or sublicense the Services except as expressly permitted; (b) reverse engineer or copy the Services; (c) exceed licensed quantities; or (d) use the Services in violation of the **AUP** (`legal/aup.md`).

## 4. Support tiers

4.1 Support is provided at the tier stated in the Order Form:

| Tier | Target first response (business hours, 08:00–22:00 Baghdad) | Channels |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | < 8 business hours | Email, in-app |
| **Priority** | < 4 business hours | Email, in-app, live chat |
| **Premier** | < 1 business hour; named contact | Email, in-app, live chat, scheduled calls |

4.2 Severity definitions and escalation follow Provider's incident process; SEV-1 handling is governed by the SLA (Exhibit A).

## 5. Fees, invoicing & taxes

5.1 Customer pays the fees in the Order Form. Unless stated otherwise, fees are invoiced **annually in advance** and due within **`[30]` days** of invoice date.

5.2 Currency is **IQD or USD** as stated in the Order Form. Fees are non-cancellable and non-refundable except as expressly provided in this MSA, the SLA, or the Refund Policy.

5.3 Taxes (VAT, withholding, and other Iraqi taxes) are additional and borne by the party legally responsible; Customer provides valid withholding certificates where applicable.

5.4 Late, undisputed amounts may accrue interest at `[the lower of 1.5%/month or the maximum permitted by law]`, and Provider may suspend on `[10]` days' notice of non-payment.

## 6. Data protection & security

6.1 The **DPA** (`legal/dpa.md`) is incorporated into and forms part of this MSA and governs the processing of Personal Data.

6.2 **Data residency options.** On request and where stated in an Order Form, Provider can offer enhanced residency (e.g., **`me-central1`-only**, no cross-region backup outside the agreed region) for sensitive Customer Data, subject to any availability or pricing terms in the Order Form.

6.3 Provider maintains the technical and organizational measures in DPA Annex II.

## 7. Audit rights (beyond the DPA)

7.1 In addition to the DPA audit right, Customer may, **once per twelve (12) months** on **30 days'** notice and subject to a mutual NDA, request reasonable evidence of Provider's compliance with this MSA (e.g., current SOC 2 report or equivalent, security questionnaire responses). On-site audits are by mutual agreement and capped at once per year absent a security incident or regulator requirement.

## 8. Confidentiality

8.1 Each party protects the other's Confidential Information as in the Terms of Service §6, which apply to this MSA *mutatis mutandis*. Pricing and Order Form terms are Provider's Confidential Information.

## 9. Warranties

9.1 Provider warrants the Services will perform materially per the Documentation and the SLA. Customer's exclusive remedy for breach of the performance warranty is the SLA service credits, and, for unresolved material non-conformity, termination of the affected Order Form with a pro-rated refund of pre-paid, unused fees.

9.2 Except as expressly stated, the Services are provided **"as is"** (Terms of Service §7.2 applies). The Services are tools and do not constitute accounting, tax, or legal advice (Terms of Service §7.3).

## 10. Indemnification

10.1 **By Provider (IP).** Provider will defend and indemnify Customer against third-party claims that the Services infringe that third party's IP rights, with the remedies in Terms of Service §9.2.

10.2 **By Customer.** Customer will defend and indemnify Provider against third-party claims arising from Customer Data or Customer's breach of the AUP or law (Terms of Service §9.1).

10.3 Procedure per Terms of Service §9.3.

## 11. Limitation of liability

11.1 Subject to §11.2, each party's aggregate liability is capped at the **greater of (a) the fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the claim under the applicable Order Form, or (b) USD 5,000**. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages or lost profits.

11.2 The cap and exclusions do not apply to Customer's payment obligations, a party's gross negligence or wilful misconduct, breach of confidentiality (other than relating to Customer Data, governed by the DPA), the §10 indemnities, or liability that cannot be limited under Iraqi law.

## 12. Insurance

12.1 Provider maintains, during the term, **Cyber Liability** and **Errors & Omissions** insurance of at least **USD 1,000,000** each (per scale-foundation R2.10 / NFR-SF.22). On request, Provider will furnish a certificate of insurance.

## 13. Term & termination

13.1 **Term.** This MSA starts on the Effective Date and continues while any Order Form is in effect.

13.2 **Termination for cause.** Either party may terminate this MSA or an affected Order Form for the other's uncured material breach (30-day cure period), or immediately on the other's insolvency.

13.3 **Termination for convenience.** Either party may terminate an Order Form for convenience on **`[60]` days'** written notice; Provider will refund pre-paid, unused subscription fees **pro-rated** to the effective date of termination (usage-based fees are non-refundable).

13.4 **Effect.** On termination, Customer's access ends; Provider makes Customer Data available for export for **30 days** then deletes it per the DPA and Privacy Policy (subject to backup rotation and legal retention).

13.5 **Survival.** Sections 5 (accrued fees), 6, 8, 9.2, 10, 11, 13.4–13.5, 14, and the DPA's surviving terms survive.

## 14. General

14.1 **Governing law & disputes.** Iraqi law governs; disputes are resolved per Terms of Service §11 (Erbil courts; arbitration for amounts > USD 50,000).

14.2 **Force majeure.** Terms of Service §12 applies.

14.3 **Order of precedence.** (1) the applicable Order Form (as limited by §2.1); (2) the DPA; (3) this MSA; (4) the SLA and other referenced policies; (5) the Terms of Service.

14.4 **Assignment, notices, severability, waiver, independent contractors** — as in Terms of Service §14.

14.5 **Entire agreement.** This MSA, its Exhibits, the DPA, the SLA, the incorporated policies, and the Order Forms are the entire agreement and supersede prior proposals.
