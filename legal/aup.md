## 1. Purpose

This policy keeps the Service safe, lawful, and reliable for all customers. By using the Service you agree to follow it. We may update it; material changes follow the modification process in the Terms of Service §13.

## 2. Prohibited activities

You must not, and must not permit anyone to:

### 2.1 Unlawful use
- Use the Service for any unlawful purpose under the laws of the **Republic of Iraq** or the **Kurdistan Region**, including **tax evasion, money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions evasion, or fraud**.
- Issue false or fraudulent invoices, falsify accounting or e-invoicing (e-Fakhata) records, or misuse the Service to deceive tax authorities or third parties.
- Infringe intellectual-property, privacy, or other rights of any person.

### 2.2 Harmful or abusive content and conduct
- Upload or transmit malware, ransomware, or other harmful code.
- Send unlawful spam or unsolicited bulk messages (including via the WhatsApp/email modules) in violation of applicable law or messaging-platform policies; you are responsible for having a lawful basis and any required consent for messages you send.
- Store or transmit content that is unlawful, defamatory, harassing, or that depicts the exploitation of minors.

### 2.3 Security and integrity
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to the Service, other tenants' data, or underlying infrastructure.
- Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Service except under an authorized, written security-testing arrangement (see §5).
- Circumvent authentication, rate limits, tenant isolation (`org_id` scoping), usage quotas, or licensing controls.
- Interfere with or disrupt the Service, e.g., denial-of-service, or impose an unreasonable or disproportionately large load.

### 2.4 Misuse of the platform
- Resell, sublicense, or provide the Service to third parties except as expressly permitted.
- Reverse engineer, decompile, or attempt to derive source code, except to the extent that restriction is prohibited by law.
- Use the Service to build a competing product, or scrape or harvest data beyond your own tenant's data.
- Share a single named-user seat among multiple individuals, or exceed licensed quantities.

## 3. Customer responsibility for data

You are responsible for the legality of the data you upload and the messages you send, and for obtaining any consents required to process personal data of your own customers, employees, and contacts (see the DPA, `legal/dpa.md`).

## 4. Enforcement

4.1 If we reasonably believe you have violated this policy, we may: investigate; remove or disable offending content; throttle or **suspend** the affected functionality or account; and, for serious or repeated violations, **terminate** the Agreement (Terms of Service §10.3 / MSA §13.2).

4.2 Where there is an imminent risk to the Service, other customers, security, or legal compliance, we may act **immediately** and notify you as soon as practicable.

4.3 We will cooperate with lawful requests from competent authorities.

## 5. Authorized security testing

Security research and penetration testing against the Service are permitted **only** under a prior written agreement (e.g., a scoped engagement or a coordinated-disclosure arrangement). Report suspected vulnerabilities to **`[security@zoho.kurd.iq]`**.

## 6. Reporting abuse

Report suspected violations to **`[abuse@zoho.kurd.iq]`** with enough detail for us to investigate.
