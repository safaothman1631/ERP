## 1. Summary

| Plan type | Refund |
|---|---|
| **Monthly plan, paid up-front** | **Full refund within 30 days** of the initial purchase |
| **Annual plan** | **Pro-rated refund** of the unused portion **after the first 30 days**; full refund if cancelled within the first 30 days |
| **Usage-based / per-transaction fees** | **Non-refundable** |
| **One-time professional / onboarding services** | Non-refundable once performed `[confirm]` |

## 2. Details

### 2.1 30-day money-back (monthly, paid up-front)
If you pay for a monthly plan up front and are not satisfied, request a refund **within 30 days** of the initial purchase and we will refund the amount paid for that plan.

### 2.2 Annual plans
- **Within the first 30 days:** full refund of the annual fee on cancellation.
- **After 30 days:** if you terminate (or we terminate without cause), we refund the **pro-rated, unused portion** of the annual fee, calculated from the effective date of termination to the end of the paid term. For enterprise customers, termination-for-convenience refunds follow **MSA §13.3**.

### 2.3 Usage-based fees
Fees charged per transaction or per unit of usage (for example, per e-invoice, per message, or per payment) reflect services already delivered and are **non-refundable**.

### 2.4 SLA service credits
Downtime remedies are handled as **service credits** under the **SLA** (`legal/sla.md`), not cash refunds.

## 3. How to request a refund

3.1 Email **`[billing@zoho.kurd.iq]`** from your account's administrator address, or use the in-app billing settings, stating your organization (`org_id`) and the charge(s) concerned.

3.2 Approved refunds are issued to the **original payment method and currency (IQD or USD)** within `[10 business days]`, subject to your payment provider's processing time. Where the original method cannot be refunded, we will agree an alternative.

3.3 Taxes already remitted to authorities are adjusted in accordance with applicable Iraqi tax rules.

## 4. Exceptions

We may decline a refund where there has been a breach of the Terms of Service or the **AUP** (`legal/aup.md`) (for example, fraud or abuse), or where the request is outside the windows above. Statutory rights that cannot be waived are unaffected.
